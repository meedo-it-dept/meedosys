Modernizing Municipal Operations

How MEEDOSys transitions Malungon from manual paper logbooks to automated digital governance.

Enterprise Operation The Traditional Approach (Paper-Based) The MEEDOSys Digital Solution
Market & Stallholder Registry Physical index cards; hard to identify vacancies or historical tenant changes. Interactive visual stall layout map with instant occupancy status and document vault.

Section F: Peace & Order (CSU)

Civil Security Unit Digital Desk

Paperless daily guard shift reports, incident tracking, offline caching, and tri-level digital signatures.

8 Core Report Modules in Every Shift Log
  1. Basic Shift Info: Auto-calculates day from date, shift hours, coverage area.
  2. Personnel Deployment: Guard IDs, assigned posts, in/out timestamps.
  3. Activities Narrative: Patrols, vendor assistance, safety sweeps.
  4. Incident Logging: Real-time incident logs with location and resolution.
  5. Violations & Apprehensions: Market code enforcement tracking.
  6. Lost & Found Registry: Found items, claim verifications.
  7. Turnover Notes: Handover instructions for incoming shift.
  8. Tri-Level Signatures: Duty Guard, Team Leader, and Market Administrator.
Offline Auto-Save Recovery
Input fields save to encrypted browser localStorage continuously. A sudden internet outage will never cause guards to lose entered activity logs or shift notes.
Guard Performance & History Search
Supervisors can search by any specific Guard ID to generate historical activity logs and attendance records across all previous shifts.
One-Click Formal Print / PDF Export
Generates formatted, ready-to-sign daily security dossiers for submission to the Municipal Mayor and Chief of Police.

The OPIF framework is built directly into the system's operational heartbeat. Because daily entries populate the backend continuously, quarterly variance percentages are computed on-the-fly.

This empowers the Municipal Mayor, MEEDO Head, and the Sangguniang Bayan to conduct data-driven policy reviews and budget allocations based on verified real-time performance indicators.

Security & Compliance

Role-Based Access & Complete Auditability

Granular departmental boundaries, user approval lifecycle, and tamper-evident event logs.

Departmental Access Control (RBAC)
Assigned Role Accessible Enterprise
Super Administrator All Sections (A through F) + User Management
Staff Section A Market Layout, Monitoring, Electric Bills
Staff Section B Slaughterhouse Intake & Reports
Staff Section C Cemetery Bookings & Calendar
Staff Section D Transport Terminal & TODA Rosters
Staff Section F CSU Peace & Order Shift Desk
User Approval Lifecycle
New users register with a requested department. Accounts remain in a Pending state and are blocked from accessing data until explicitly approved by the MEEDO Administrator.
Immutable Audit Trail
Every critical event (user logins, edits, bill generations, payments, cemetery schedules) is permanently recorded with timestamp and acting username in the immutable Log sheet.
